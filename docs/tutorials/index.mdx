# Tutorials

Tutorials are lessons. Each one takes you through a complete, safe exercise, from an empty machine to a working result, so that you get familiar with a tool by using it. Follow the steps in order: you don't need to understand everything yet, the [explanation](/docs/explanation) section is there for that.

| Tutorial | What you will achieve | You need | Time |
|----------|-----------------------|----------|------|
| [Your first PiRogue on Debian](/docs/tutorials/pirogue-on-debian) | Turn a Debian 12 machine into a PiRogue and open its dashboard | A Debian 12 machine or virtual machine | 10 min |
| [Your first PiRogue on a Raspberry Pi](/docs/tutorials/pirogue-on-a-raspberry-pi) | Flash PiRogue OS, boot it and connect your first device | A Raspberry Pi, a micro SD card, an Ethernet cable | 1 h |
| [Capture and analyze the traffic of a device](/docs/tutorials/capture-and-analyze-traffic) | Record the traffic of a phone and explore it in Wireshark | A PiRogue in access point mode, a phone | 30 min, then a few days of capture |
| [Try Colander on your computer](/docs/tutorials/colander-on-your-computer) | Run the whole Colander stack locally and open your first case | Docker with Docker Compose | 15 min |
| [Try Threatr on your computer](/docs/tutorials/threatr-on-your-computer) | Run Threatr and ask it about an indicator | Docker with Docker Compose | 5 min |
| [Try Mandolin on your computer](/docs/tutorials/mandolin-on-your-computer) | Run Mandolin and analyze your first files | Docker with Docker Compose | 5 min |

Once you have completed a tutorial, the [how-to guides](/docs/how-to) help you with real tasks, such as [installing a PiRogue in production](/docs/how-to/pirogue/install-pirogue) or [deploying Colander for your team](/docs/how-to/colander/deploy-with-ansible).
