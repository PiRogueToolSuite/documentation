# Build the PiRogue hardware

This guide shows how to print the case of a Raspberry Pi-based PiRogue and assemble it with its HAT. The parts and the specifications are listed in [Hardware](/docs/reference/pirogue/hardware). If you don't want to build it yourself, [reach out to the PTS team](https://pts-project.org/contact/).

## Print the case

You need:

* the [manufacturing files of the case](https://github.com/PiRogueToolSuite/pirogue-case/tree/main/v1.0_beta): either the 4 separate STL files, or the one containing the 4 parts ready to slice
* a 3D printer
* PLA or PETG

Slice the files with the [3D printing parameters](/docs/reference/pirogue/hardware#3d-printing-parameters) and print them. A complete case prints in around 3 hours.

## Get the HAT

Have the HAT manufactured from its [KiCad project and manufacturing files](https://github.com/PiRogueToolSuite/pirogue-hat/tree/main/v1.0_beta), with the parts listed in [Manufacturing files](/docs/reference/pirogue/hardware#manufacturing-files).

## Assemble the PiRogue

Gather the [assembly parts](/docs/reference/pirogue/hardware#assembly-parts), then:

* Secure the fan in place with 4 M2.5 12mm bolts and 4 M2.5 nuts.
* Place the 4 standoffs between the Raspberry Pi and the HAT.
