# Share a case with a team

In Colander, cases are shared with teams: a team is the only way to give other users access to a case. This guide shows how to create a team, add contributors to it, and share a case with it.

## Create a team
Go to *Colander* > *Collaborate* > *Teams* and give the team a name.

<CaptionedImage src="/img/colander/collaboration/team-1.png" alt="Team creation form" caption="Team creation form" align="center"/>

## Add contributors
Once the team is created, click *Details* to add a contributor. Since a Colander instance can host multiple users from different organizations, users cannot search for another user by their name. Instead, the owner of the team has to ask other users for their *Contributor ID*. This way, they are free to decide whether they want to share their ID or not. The *Contributor ID* can be found in user's profile.

<CaptionedImage src="/img/colander/collaboration/team-2.png" alt="Overview of the form for adding a contributor to a team" caption="Overview of the form for adding a contributor to a team" align="center"/>

:::caution[Ownership]

Only the owner of the team can:
* edit the team
* delete the team
* add or remove contributors

:::


## Share the case
Only the owner of a case can share it with a team they are either owner or contributor. To share a case with one or multiple teams, simply edit the case and select/deselect the teams you want to share the case with.

<CaptionedImage src="/img/colander/collaboration/case-team.png" alt="Form allowing to share a case with one or many teams" caption="Form allowing to share a case with one or many teams" align="center"/>
