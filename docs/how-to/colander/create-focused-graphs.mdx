# Create focused graphs of a case

This guide shows how to create sub-graphs: additional graphs of a case that show a projection of its knowledge, such as a single attack vector or the infrastructure of a threat actor. A sub-graph shows the entities and relations of its case, so the knowledge is never duplicated. To learn why Colander represents an investigation as a graph, see [Knowledge graph](/docs/explanation/colander/knowledge-graph).

## Create a sub-graph

1. Open a case and go to the *Graph* workspace.
2. Select **Sub Graphs** in the workspace menu.
3. Give the sub-graph a meaningful name and, optionally, a description.
4. Save it. The sub-graph appears in the list of sub-graphs of the case.

Sub-graphs are personal: each user only sees the sub-graphs they created.

## Lay out a sub-graph

Open the sub-graph from the list. It opens in the [graph editor](/docs/reference/colander/graph-editor), with the same mouse and keyboard actions as the main graph. The layout of a sub-graph is saved separately from the layout of the main graph, together with a thumbnail of the sub-graph, displayed in the list.

## Pin a sub-graph

Pin the sub-graphs you use often: pinned sub-graphs are listed in the menu of the *Graph* workspace. Pin or unpin a sub-graph with its pin button.

## Delete a sub-graph

Delete a sub-graph from the list of sub-graphs. Its entities and relations stay in the case.
